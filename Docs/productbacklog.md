# Suggested MVP (Sprint 1–3)

The following stories provide a solid minimum viable product:

### Sprint 1 – Customer Onboarding

* US-101 Register Customer
* US-102 Login
* US-201 Browse Policies
* US-203 Purchase Policy
* US-501 Policy Purchase Payment

### Sprint 2 – Premium Management

* US-301 View Premium Schedule
* US-302 Pay Premium
* US-303 Premium History
* US-503 Generate Receipt
* US-901 Premium Reminder

### Sprint 3 – Claims

* US-401 Register Claim
* US-402 Upload Documents
* US-404 Verify Claim
* US-407 Approve/Reject Claim
* US-408 Claim Settlement

### Sprint 4 – Agent Operations

* US-601 Register Customer
* US-603 Sell Policy
* US-604 Premium Follow-up
* US-607 Commission Tracking

### Sprint 5 – Management & Reporting

* US-701 Policy Approval
* US-702 Claim Approval
* US-704 Premium Monitoring
* US-707 Operational Reports
* US-708 Business Dashboard

This backlog contains approximately **55 user stories** grouped into **10 epics**, providing a comprehensive roadmap for developing the TFL Insurance Management System using ASP.NET Core Web API and an agile Scrum approach.
